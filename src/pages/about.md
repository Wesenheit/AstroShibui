 ## hi!

My name is Mateusz, I am a PhD student at the ELLIS Insitute Tübingen/Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, supervised 
by [Maximilian Dax](https://max-dax.github.io/). I am specializing in probabilistic inference for physical systems, recently related to the detection of the gravitational waves.
Most of my research is centered around the simulation-based inference, deep probabilistic modeling and everything bayesian. 
Moreover, I have great interest in the HPC and distributed computing.

Previously, I was employed at the ASML facility at Veldhoven where I was working on  various (bayesian) tools for the diagnostics
of the NXE machines.
Most of my work was centered around the research/implementation/scaling of various ML models coupled with physical ray-tracer simulations.


## before
I graduated from the University of Warsaw with a Physics degree (specialization in astrophysics)
where I studied as part of the College of Interdisciplinary studies. Hence,
I also have a minor in Math/Computer Science and I was specializing in HPC/ML.
I used to work in the field of observational (gravitational microlensing) and theoretical/computational astrophysics (plasma physics, accrection, jets) before switching gears to applied ML.
